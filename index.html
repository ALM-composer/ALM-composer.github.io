Welcome!

I am a Danish composer and multi-instrumentalist working with open scores, experimental composition and improvisation, often combining acoustic and electronic instruments. I am most interested in the space between composed form and spontaneous process, and in the unexpected relationships that can emerge between musicians.

Recent projects include a choreographed piece for six guitars (Guitarden of Eden), music for a clarinet choir and electronics (Jeux d'eau), and a work for an ensemble playing 3D-printed organ pipes (Multiple Pipes for Major Players). This site gathers my works and recordings. I hope you enjoy exploring it.
