Anders Lauge Meldgaard · works · about

About

Composer and multi-instrumentalist Anders Lauge Meldgaard works in a range of musical expressions, including aleatoric composition, improvisation and open-ended scores, often including himself as performer in his pieces. His music resists easy categorization, and is often described as playful, innovative, conceptually driven, unpredictable and kaleidoscopic. Being less concerned with stylistic allegiance than with the interplay of materials, the music of ALM is a place where acoustic and electronic instruments unfolds as a series of sonic proposals, with equal parts formal experiment and intuitive drift, where structure and spontaneity are in constant negotiation.

His most recent works include Jeux d’eau (2025), The Art of Playing the Fantasia (2023) for Electronic Woodwind Instrument and eurorack synthesizer, released by Los Angeles based label Sun Ark and Spirit (2025) composed for the string trio Halvcirkel and ALM performing on New Ondomo (a Japanese version of an Ondes Martenot) and electronics. He is a founding member of record label and experimental music collective År & Dag based in Copenhagen, Denmark. His works has been performed internationally, with appearances at Novas Frequências in Brazil, SuperDeluxe (JP), Grimm Museum in Berlin, Roskilde Festival (DK), Muziekgebouw (NL), Museum of Contemporary Art (DK), among other.

Om

Komponist og multiinstrumentalist Anders Lauge Meldgaard arbejder i et bredt felt af musikalske udtryk, lige fra moderne kompositionsmusik til fri improvisation over lydinstallationer, elektronisk musik, sangskrivning sat i orkestrale midi collager, spraglede mixtapes og støjende værker for elektrisk guitar sekstet. Spændingen mellem komposition og improvisation bruges ofte som motiverende drivkraft bag de musikalske eventyr. Er autodidakt komponist, og har studeret saxofon på Rytmisk Musikkonservatorium i København. Har tidligere optrådt og udgivet anmelderroste albums under aliaset Frisk Frugt. Hans musikalske univers er blevet vist og fremført både nationalt og internationalt på bl.a. Novas Frequências (Rio de Janeiro), SuperDeluxe (Tokyo), The Living Art Museum (Reykjavik), Cafe Oto (London), Grimm Museum (Berlin), Muziekgebouw (Amsterdam), By:Larm (Oslo), Inkonst (Malmö), Roskilde Festival og Click Festival, samt på turnér i Europa, USA og Asien. Han er stiftende medlem af pladeselskabet og den eksperimenterende musik og kompositionssammenslutning År & Dag.
