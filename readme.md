A personal website for Copenhagen based composer and musician Anders Lauge Meldgaard. 

Composer and multi-instrumentalist Anders Lauge Meldgaard works in a range of musical expressions, including aleatoric composition, improvisation and open-ended scores, often including himself as performer in his pieces. His music resists easy categorization, and is often described as playful, innovative, conceptually driven, unpredictable and kaleidoscopic. Being less concerned with stylistic allegiance than with the interplay of materials, the music of ALM is a place where acoustic and electronic instruments unfolds as a series of sonic proposals, with equal parts formal experiment and intuitive drift, where structure and spontaneity are in constant negotiation. His most recent works include Jeux d’eau (2025), The Art of Playing the Fantasia (2023) for Electronic Woodwind Instrument and eurorack synthesizer, released by Los Angeles based label Sun Ark and Spirit (2025) composed for the string trio Halvcirkel  and ALM performing on New Ondomo (a Japanese version of an Ondes Martenot) and electronics. He is a founding member of record label and experimental music collective År & Dag based in Copenhagen, Denmark.
