Anders Lauge Meldgaard & Halvcirkel
"SPRIT"
Japan Tour April 2025

Greetings, I'm Anders Lauge Meldgaard, a Danish composer and multi-instrumentalist based in Copenhagen. Over the years, I've had the pleasure of collaborating with the esteemed string ensemble Halvcirkel. Our journey together began with humble origins, as I crafted songs and composed simple string arrangements for them to perform. As our partnership gradually flourished, we embarked on ambitious projects, including compositions for ensembles of 8 and 12 musicians. Examples of this can be found on the two releases "At synge verden ind i en ny og mangfoldig tid" and "12 instrumenter til Henning", the latter being a tribute to the late great Danish Fluxus artist and composer Henning Christiansen.Our shared energy ultimately led to Halvcirkel commissioning a piece from me, which we titled "Fragment 94" and released in 2021. However, when time came for Halvcirkel to perform this piece, Bettina, one of the group's violinists, was on maternity leave. In her absence, Mika, the viola player, approached me with the opportunity to step in and fill the void. Admittedly, the idea of performing alongside such talented string players left me feeling a bit nervous. Yet, with Mika's encouragement, I embraced the challenge. Coincidentally, I had recently acquired a new instrument called New Ondomo, a Japanese iteration of the Ondes Martenot—an instrument pioneered by the French cellist and radio engineer, Maurice Martenot, nearly a century ago. This innovative instrument, akin to a single-stringed cello or violin, cracefully combined with Halvcirkel's sound, enhancing our musical synergy.

Our most recent collaboration has yielded a 45-minute-long piece titled "Spirit." This work is slated for release late 2024 / early 2025. In this new piece Anders Lauge Meldgaard assumes dual roles as both composer and performing musician on New Ondomo and electronics, further developing the collective experiences shared while collaborating on Fragment 94.

Spirit is a series of open-ended compositions and frames for improvisation, arranged in collaboration with Halvcirkel. The music features a specific instrumentation including violin, viola, cello, new ondomo and electronics. Over the past six years of our collaboration, a profound evolution has unfolded in the dynamic relationship between composer and ensemble. "Spirit" stands as a testament to our renewed collaborative work, pushing the boundaries of previous compositional and improvisational concepts and ideas further on.

Halvcirkel
String quartet based in Copenhagen since 2015, mainly doing modern composition and classical music in experimental formats. Halvcirkel has for the past years worked with improvisation as an artistic tool and expression. In their repertoire they’ve been playing composers such as Terry Riley, Arvo Pärt, Philip Glass, Shostakovich, Benjamin Britten, Maurice Ravel and the quartet have had closer collaborations with Copenhagen based composers such as Signe Lykke, Anders Lauge Meldgaard and Nick Martin. Halvcirkel released two albums, Halvcirkel 2017 (LP), The Gift 2018 (CD) on their own label Weltfreude Music and participated as sideband in many projects around in Copenhagen.
Anders Lauge Meldgaard
Composer and multi-instrumentalist who works in a wide field of musical expressions, ranging from contemporary music to free improvisation over sound installations, songwriting, orchestral collages, chamber music, mixtapes, noisy works for electric guitar sextet and lately also electronic music. The tension between composition and improvisation is often used as a motivating force to propel his musical adventures. He has formerly performed and released critically acclaimed albums under the alias Frisk Frugt. His works has been performed internationally, with appearances at Novas Frequências in Brazil, SuperDeluxe (JP), Grimm Museum in Berlin, Roskilde Festival (DK), Muziekgebouw (NL), Museum of Contemporary Art (DK), and many other places. He is a founding member of Copenhagen based music circle and record label År & Dag.
