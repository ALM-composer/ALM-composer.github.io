Lille Solstråle Sad Og Så På Månen · 2025 →

Lille Solstråle Sad Og Så På Månen (eng. Little Sunbeam Sat and Looked at the Moon) is a contemporary choral work I composed at the intersection of experimental choral music, sound art, and visual art, in collaboration with visual artist Lise Haller Baggesen and the choir BARK, conducted by Lasse Aagaard. The work was commissioned by the Museum of Contemporary Art, Roskilde, and premiered at the PLATFORM stage during Roskilde Festival 2025. It was later presented as a sound and textile installation at the former Kurhus building at Sct. Hans Hospital, as part of the exhibition Kaååråålines Vers in autumn 2025. At the invitation of the museum, Lise Haller Baggesen initiated the project through research into texts and handwritten notes left by Karoline Ebbesen (1852–1936), a visual artist and patient at Sct. Hans Hospital from the 1880s until her death. My composition takes its point of departure in Ebbesen’s rhythmic and poetic texts, which I translate into a contemporary choral work. I approach the many voices of BARK as a collective resonant space, allowing the voice of a historically marginalised artist to emerge and be heard in new ways. We are currently working on developing the songs into an album release, which we hope to realise in 2027. Together with choir director Lasse Aagaard, I am exploring how the material can evolve beyond its original live and installation contexts and take shape as a musical release. The process draws on both the live performances at Roskilde Festival and the sound installation presented at Kaååråålines Vers, allowing me to revisit, refine and further develop the compositions while retaining the distinctive character of the original work. The album will bring the songs together in a new format, creating an opportunity for the work to exist independently of the exhibition and performance settings in which it first emerged. We are also considering accompanying the release with a booklet containing lyrics, visual material, and an introduction to Karoline Ebbesen’s life and work. I see the album as an opportunity to extend the life of the project and create a space for listeners to engage more deeply with Ebbesen’s poetic universe, while bringing together music, text and visual material in a lasting form.
